About Us

Built for specialized component manufacturing and technical R&D.

Golden Steed relies on a professional manufacturing and supply chain team, plus industrial resources in the power sector. Its standardized operating system covers manufacturing, quality control, work safety and process management.

The company supports customers from technology R&D and tooling design to precision forming, process optimization and delivery. Its values are ultimate execution, agility, innovation and win-win cooperation.

Manufacturing Capabilities

One-stop support from rough machining to precision forming.

Core processes include copper and aluminum hot forging, stamping and drawing, CNC precision machining, laser welding, copper busbar manufacturing, harness assembly and cabinet integration.

01

Stamping & Welding

315T and 400T stamping capacity, blanking, drawing, flattening, MIG, MAG and TIG welding for structural components.

02

CNC Machining & Turning

Machining centers, CNC turning and precision cutting support copper, aluminum and custom mechanical components.

03

Copper Busbar

Three-dimensional copper busbar processing with automated positioning, bending and supporting production equipment.

04

Electrical Harness Assembly

Harness preparation, welding, crimping and testing for VCB mechanisms and electrical control assemblies.

05

Cabinet Assembly & Testing

OEM cabinet assembly for high-voltage, medium-voltage, control, intelligent, inverter and customized systems.

06

Measuring Equipment

Automatic image measuring, profile and surface roughness equipment support consistent inspection and quality control.
